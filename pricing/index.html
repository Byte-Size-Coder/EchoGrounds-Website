Flexible Pricing

Pricing tailored to your community's needs

We understand that funding models vary by community. Pricing will be discussed during your appointment and can be structured as an annual subscription, multi-year license, or one-time payment with ongoing support costs. All options include core platform functionality with transparent high-compute caveats.

Community-Wide License

Annual Subscription

Pay yearly with maximum flexibility.

  • One subscription for the entire community, not per user or per seat
  • Unlimited users
  • Unlimited language content
  • All current features
  • All future non-AI features
  • Secure Azure Canada hosting and backups
  • Community data ownership
  • Virtual onboarding, email support, quarterly check-ins, and funding guidance

Pricing will be discussed during your appointment based on community size and needs.

Multi-Year License

Lock in stable, predictable costs across funding cycles.

  • 2-year or 3-year licensing options available
  • Designed to align with ILCP or other multi-year funding cycles
  • Supports long-term community planning and delivery
  • Creates stable, predictable budgeting across funding years
  • Includes everything in the annual subscription model
  • Potential discounts for multi-year commitments

Pricing will be discussed during your appointment based on license duration and community size.

One-Time Payment + Support

Make a single upfront investment in your platform.

  • One-time license fee for permanent platform access
  • Annual support and maintenance costs (optional)
  • Unlimited users and language content
  • All current features included
  • Future non-AI features included
  • Secure Azure Canada hosting and backups
  • Community data ownership
  • Email support and quarterly check-ins available

Pricing will be discussed during your appointment and tailored to your community's long-term support needs.

What is always included

All platform features All future non-AI features Unlimited users Unlimited content Unlimited languages Community branding Secure hosting Backups Updates Support

EchoGrounds will never charge extra for core functionality.
